INTERNAL TECHNOLOGY BRIEF - UTILITY-SCALE SOLAR PHOTOVOLTAICS
Revision 4, Q2 planning cycle

Cost position.
Utility-scale PV reached a global weighted-average LCOE of USD 0.043/kWh in 2024, down
roughly 90% from 2010. Module prices fell below USD 0.09/W in late 2024 on the back of
Chinese polysilicon oversupply. Balance-of-system and soft costs now dominate project
budgets in mature markets - in the US they are approximately 65% of installed cost.

Technology mix.
Monocrystalline PERC still ships in volume, but TOPCon overtook it in new capacity during
2024 and now holds the majority of tier-1 production lines. Heterojunction (HJT) holds a
smaller premium niche. Perovskite-silicon tandems have passed 33.9% cell efficiency in the
laboratory; commercial durability under damp-heat cycling remains the blocker, with no
bankable 25-year warranty yet offered.

Capacity factors and siting.
Typical fixed-tilt capacity factor is 16-20%; single-axis tracking raises this to 22-28% in
high-irradiance regions. Agrivoltaics and floating PV are the two siting innovations with
material land-constraint relief; floating PV installations exceeded 7 GW cumulative in 2024.

Known limitations of this brief.
Concentrated solar power (CSP) with thermal storage is not assessed here - see the separate
thermal generation file. Distributed rooftop economics are out of scope.
