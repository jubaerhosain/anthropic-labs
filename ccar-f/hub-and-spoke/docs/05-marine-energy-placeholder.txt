INTERNAL MEMO - MARINE AND OCEAN ENERGY

Status: PLACEHOLDER. No technology assessment has been commissioned for this category.

Marine energy (tidal stream, tidal range/barrage, wave, ocean thermal, salinity gradient)
was scoped out of the current review cycle on the grounds of immaterial installed capacity
in our operating regions. Global installed marine capacity is understood to be under 1 GW.

An assessment should be commissioned before the next planning cycle. Until then this file
should not be cited as evidence that marine energy is unimportant - only that we have not
looked at it. External sources will be required.
