INTERNAL MEMO - FRONTIER AND PRE-COMMERCIAL TECHNOLOGIES

Scope note: this memo lists frontier technologies for tracking purposes only. None are
assessed in depth. Each line is a pointer, not a finding.

- Fusion. Excluded from the renewables assessment on definitional grounds (not renewable in
  the conventional taxonomy; fuel is mined lithium and deuterium). Tracking only. Private
  sector funding has been substantial. NO TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT ON FILE - external sourcing
  required if fusion is to be covered.
- Green hydrogen and derived e-fuels. Electrolyser cost curves tracked separately.
- Space-based solar power. Watching brief; no near-term relevance.
- Osmotic / salinity-gradient power. Watching brief.
- Advanced nuclear (SMRs). Out of scope - not renewable, tracked by the nuclear team.

Reviewer comment appended to revision 2: "The exclusion of fusion on definitional grounds is
defensible for a renewables-only portfolio review but will look like an oversight in any
document framed as covering energy technologies broadly. Flag it explicitly rather than
letting the absence speak."
